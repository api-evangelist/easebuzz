# Easebuzz

> Easebuzz is a leading Indian full-stack digital payments and fintech platform trusted by 250,000+ merchants across industries. Its RBI-authorized and PCI DSS-compliant payment gateway supports 150+ payment modes for both online and offline payment collections, including UPI, cards, net banking, wallets, EMI, and BNPL. Known for high transaction success rates, easy integration, quick onboarding, and dedicated customer support,  Pricing starts at 1.5% per transaction with no setup fees.

- [EasyCollect](https://easebuzz.in/easy-collect/): Subscription-based payment management — manage recurring billing and collections via API.
- [InstaCollect](https://easebuzz.in/insta-collect/): Instant collections using Virtual Payment Addresses (VPA) — real-time fund collection via UPI.
- [EBPS – Easebuzz Bill Payment System](https://easebuzz.in/ebps-easebuzz-bill-payment-system/): Bill payment system for service providers and utilities to collect payments from customers.
- [Shopify Payment Gateway](https://easebuzz.in/shopify-payment-gateway/): Easebuzz payment gateway plugin for Shopify stores — enables UPI, cards, netbanking, and wallets at checkout.
- [Affordability Suite](https://easebuzz.in/affordability-suite/): EMI, Buy Now Pay Later, and other affordability options to improve checkout conversion.
- [Offer Engine](https://easebuzz.in/offer-engine/): Configure and manage discount coupons, cashback, and promotional offers at checkout.
